Format: `[PF-xx] Title — author/publisher, year — URL — status — what it supports`

Status legend:
- **verified**: page/file fetched and the cited content read.
- **snippet-only**: only a search-result snippet or bibliographic record seen; full text not read.
- **uncertain/conflicting**: read, but contradicted elsewhere or ambiguous.

## IEEE PES test feeders

- [PF-01] IEEE PES Test Feeder Working Group — Resources page — IEEE PES, page © 2026 — https://cmte.ieee.org/pes-testfeeders/resources/ — **verified** — Lists the feeders (4, 13, 34, 37, 123, 8500-node, NEV, LVNTS, European LV) and the download URLs under `/wp-content/uploads/sites/167/2017/08/`. The site footer reads "© Copyright 2026 IEEE – All rights reserved" and "Use of this website signifies your agreement to the IEEE Terms and Conditions". The page gives no open licence for the data files.
- [PF-02] "IEEE 13 Node Test Feeder" (`feeder13.zip`: Word .doc plus .xls data files) — IEEE PES Distribution System Analysis Subcommittee. The RDAP printout inside is dated 6-24-2004. https://cmte.ieee.org/pes-testfeeders/wp-content/uploads/sites/167/2017/08/feeder13.zip — **verified** (downloaded; text pulled from the .doc with `strings`) — Line configurations 601–607 (Z in Ω/mile, B in µS/mile), line segments, loads, capacitors, regulator settings, and the published "Power-Flow Results": the radial flow summary, the voltage profile and the regulator taps.
- [PF-03] "IEEE 4 Node Test Feeder", revised Sept. 19, 2006 (`feeder4.zip`) — IEEE PES DSASC — https://cmte.ieee.org/pes-testfeeders/wp-content/uploads/sites/167/2017/08/feeder4.zip — **verified** (downloaded; the solution tables are embedded Excel objects, pulled out with olefile+xlrd) — Transformer test data (6000 kVA 12.47/4.16 kV and 12.47/24.9 kV, R=1%, X=6%), loads, and solution tables for the GrY-GrY, GrY-D, Y-D, D-GrY, D-D and open GrY-D connections, each step-down/step-up and balanced/unbalanced. The published tables contain typos (see 04-power-flow.md §1.3).
- [PF-04] W. H. Kersting, "Radial distribution test feeders", IEEE Trans. Power Systems 6(3):975–985, 1991, doi:10.1109/59.119237 — IEEE (Distribution Planning WG report) — https://manuscript.isc.ac/Inventory/49/1175778.htm (bibliographic record) — **snippet-only** — Original publication of the 4-wire wye and 3-wire delta test feeders. Search snippets disagree on the year (1991 or 1992): the journal metadata says 1991.
- [PF-05] K. P. Schneider et al., "Analytic Considerations and Design Basis for the IEEE Distribution Test Feeders", IEEE Trans. Power Systems 33(3):3181–3188, 2018, doi:10.1109/TPWRS.2017.2760011 — accepted manuscript at OSTI — https://www.osti.gov/pages/servlets/purl/1432185 — **verified** (PDF text extracted) — States the purpose of each feeder (4-node: transformer connections; 13-node: convergence on a very unbalanced system; 34-node: long feeder with regulators; 37-node: 3-wire delta UG; 123-node: multiple regulators and capacitors). Published solutions were "verified by at least two TFWG members, each using a separate simulation package, achieving voltage solution values within 0.01%". Also covers sweep, impedance, NR and TCIM methods ("Fast decoupled load flow is not possible because of high R/X ratios"), and the change in what a "node" means.
- [PF-06] OpenDSS IEEE 13-node model `IEEE13Nodeckt.dss` + `IEEELineCodes.DSS` — EPRI OpenDSS SVN (script credited to Tennessee Tech students, 2009) — https://sourceforge.net/p/electricdss/code/HEAD/tree/trunk/Distrib/IEEETestCases/13Bus/ — **verified** (downloaded and run) — The model differs from the published case on purpose: the substation transformer impedance is divided by 1000 and the source angle is advanced 30°. Its comments include an "alternate solution script" that fixes the taps at the published values.

## Methods

- [PF-16] W. H. Kersting, *Distribution System Modeling and Analysis*, 4th ed., CRC Press, 2017 (ISBN 9781498772150) — https://oreilly.com/library/view/distribution-system-modeling/9781498772150 — **snippet-only** (book not read; 403 on the chapter page) — Ch. 9 "Load Models"; Ch. 10 "Distribution Feeder Analysis" with "The Ladder Iterative Technique" (linear and nonlinear networks, unbalanced three-phase feeder); modified ladder technique in §6.4.
- [PF-17] Q. Zhang, Z. Wang, "Power Flow Calculation in Distribution Systems", EE 653 lecture slides, Iowa State Univ. — https://wzy.ece.iastate.edu/PPT/EE653%20Power%20Flow%20in%20Distribution%20Systems.pdf — **verified** (PDF text extracted) — Ladder method after Kersting 4th ed (example tolerance 0.0001), BIBC/BCBV direct method and its weakly-meshed extension, and claims about NR/GS ill-conditioning for distribution (high R/X, radial structure), citing Cheng & Shirmohammadi 1995.
- [PF-18] D. Shirmohammadi, H. W. Hong, A. Semlyen, G. X. Luo, "A compensation-based power flow method for weakly meshed distribution and transmission networks", IEEE TPWRS 3(2):753–762, May 1988, doi:10.1109/59.192932 — **snippet-only** — Branch-current sweep with multiport compensation for loops. Handles 3-phase unbalanced, but the paper details single-phase only.
- [PF-19] C. S. Cheng, D. Shirmohammadi, "A three-phase power flow method for real-time distribution system analysis", IEEE TPWRS 10(2):671–679, May 1995 — **snippet-only** (cited in PF-17) — Three-phase compensation-based sweep.
- [PF-20] J.-H. Teng, "A direct approach for distribution system load flow solutions", IEEE TPWRD 18(3):882–887, Jul 2003, doi:10.1109/TPWRD.2003.813818 — **snippet-only** — BIBC/BCBV matrices; no LU or Jacobian; extension to loops.
- [PF-21] P. A. N. Garcia, J. L. R. Pereira, S. Carneiro Jr., V. M. da Costa, N. Martins, "Three-phase power flow calculations using the current injection method", IEEE TPWRS 15(2):508–514, May 2000, doi:10.1109/59.867133 — **snippet-only** — TCIM: rectangular current-injection NR of order 6n, with a Jacobian of the same structure as Ybus.

## Browser / JS

- [PF-27] ECMAScript® 2026 Language Specification (living draft at tc39.es), §6.1.6.1 Number type operations — https://tc39.es/ecma262/multipage/ecmascript-data-types-and-values.html — **verified** — Number::add (§6.1.6.1.7), ::multiply (§6.1.6.1.4) and ::divide (§6.1.6.1.5) are defined "according to the rules of IEEE 754-2019 binary double-precision arithmetic". Rounding of the mathematical value to Number "corresponds exactly to the behaviour of the IEEE 754-2019 roundTiesToEven mode". Number::exponentiate (`**`, Math.pow) "returns an implementation-approximated value".
- [PF-28] ECMAScript spec §21.3.2 "Function Properties of the Math Object" — https://tc39.es/ecma262/multipage/numbers-and-dates.html — **verified** — "The behaviour of the functions acos, acosh, asin, asinh, atan, atanh, atan2, cbrt, cos, cosh, exp, expm1, hypot, log, log1p, log2, log10, pow, random, sin, sinh, tan, and tanh is not precisely specified here…"; fdlibm use is "recommended (but not specified)". Math.sqrt (§21.3.2.33) returns "𝔽(the square root of ℝ(n))", which is exactly rounded and not implementation-approximated. Math.random (§21.3.2.28) uses an "implementation-defined algorithm".
- [PF-29] bryc, "PRNGs.md" (JS PRNG collection) — https://github.com/bryc/code/blob/master/jshash/PRNGs.md — **verified** — sfc32 (128-bit state, "passes PractRand, as well as Crush/BigCrush"), mulberry32 (32-bit state, period about 2^32, "passes gjrand"; may skip about 1/3 of 32-bit outputs), xoshiro128** ("poor randomness in the lower bits"). Licence: "Public domain".
- [PF-30] S. Vigna, "xoshiro / xoroshiro generators and the PRNG shootout" — https://prng.di.unimi.it/ — **verified** — Describes xoshiro128** as the 32-bit all-purpose generator and recommends seeding with SplitMix. The page fetched does not state a licence for the reference code.
- [PF-31] npm registry metadata (latest versions): mathjs 15.2.0 Apache-2.0; ml-matrix 6.15.1 MIT; complex.js 2.4.3 MIT; ndarray 1.1.1 MIT; numeric 1.2.6 (no licence field) — https://registry.npmjs.org/<pkg>/latest — **verified**.
- [PF-32] math.js docs, `math.slu` — https://mathjs.org/docs/reference/functions/slu.html — **verified** — "Calculate the Sparse Matrix LU decomposition with full pivoting". Parameters `order` (0–3) and `threshold`. The page gives no information on whether complex entries are supported.
