## Transformer thermal model and ageing

- **[TH-01]** IEC 60076-7 Ed. 2.0 2018-01 (dated "IEC 60076-7:2017") *Power transformers – Part 7: Loading guide for mineral-oil-immersed power transformers*, publisher preview (cover, foreword, full table of contents) — IEC via VDE Verlag, 2018 — https://assets.vde-verlag.de/iec-normen/preview-pdf/info_iec60076-7%7Bed2.0%7Den.pdf — **verified (structure only)** — Supports: the edition exists and replaced the 2005 edition. It lists the 2018 changes: insulation life updated, temperature limits reviewed, maximum core temperature added, thermal models rewritten. It gives the 2018 table numbering: Table 1 ageing rates, Table 2 temperature limits, Table 3 current limits, Table 4 thermal characteristics, Table 5 enclosure correction. The worked examples are now Annex H (exponential, 250 MVA, time periods 0–190–365–500–705–730–745 min), Annex I (difference equations, Tables I.1 and I.2) and Annex K (overload tables). **The preview has no numeric table values.**

## Cables

  - cites IEC 60364-5-54 and IEC 60949

## Insulation ageing, diagnostics, SF6

- **[TH-21]** "Dissolved gas analysis" — Wikipedia, accessed 2026-11-13 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dissolved_gas_analysis — **verified** — Supports: the key-gas to fault mapping (H2 for PD; C2H2 for arcing; C2H4 for thermal; CH4 and C2H6 for oil overheating; CO and CO2 for cellulose); the Duval triangle concept (CH4, C2H4, C2H2 percentages); that IEC 60599 and IEEE C57.104 are the guiding standards (IEEE C57.104-2019 cited). **Zone boundaries are not given.**
- **[TH-22]** "Partial discharge" — Wikipedia, accessed 2026-11-13 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_discharge — **verified** — Supports: IEC 60270:2000 is the PD measurement standard; apparent charge is measured in pC; TEV (discovered 1974, EA Technology) is measured in mV or dBmV with no panel removal, but "sensitivity is not likely to be sufficient" for solid-dielectric cable systems; ultrasonic sensing (40 kHz airborne; 20–300 kHz contact); online monitoring with HFCT and UHF.
- **[TH-23]** "Electrical treeing" — Wikipedia, accessed 2026-11-13 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_treeing — **verified** — Supports: water trees (diffuse, partially conductive, need water and field, start at defects) are "not generally a reliability concern unless they are able to initiate an electrical tree"; electrical trees grow through repeated PD and are accelerated by switching transients; treeing has been a known failure mechanism since 1969; trees are detectable by PD measurement. Mitigation is not covered.
- **[TH-24]** Regulation (EU) 2024/573 on fluorinated greenhouse gases — EUR-Lex — https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/573/oj/eng and https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=OJ:L_202400573 — **failed (both fetches returned empty)** — Not verified. The SF6 MV-switchgear phase-out dates must not be used until this is read.
