## Reliability indices

- [OP-01] *Reliability Metrics and Reliability Value-Based Planning*, slide deck (Joseph H. Eto, Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory; Midwest PUC training, Jan 2018). https://eta-publications.lbl.gov/sites/default/files/7._eto_-_reliability_metrics_and_rvbp.pdf. **verified** (PDF text extracted). Supports:
  - Slide 3: SAIDI, SAIFI, CAIDI = SAIDI/SAIFI, and MAIFI ("< 5min each").
  - Slide 4: EIA 2015 SAIDI/SAIFI by utility type.
  - Slide 5: IEEE 1366 history (1998, MED segmentation added 2003, amended 2012 with the MED definition unchanged), T_MED = exp(α+2.5β), 99.379 %, about 2.3 MEDs per year.
  - EPB Chattanooga automation: SAIDI 112 → 61.8 min, SAIFI 1.42 → 0.69, $5.6 M investment, $26.8 M benefits.
  - Slide 16: interruption-cost table (ICE).
  - Caveat: the "≥" symbol before "5min" on slide 3 was lost in extraction.
- [OP-02] *RIIO-ED2 Regulatory Instructions and Guidance – Annex F: Interruptions v1.1* (Ofgem, Feb 2025). https://www.ofgem.gov.uk/sites/default/files/2025-02/RIIO-ED2-Annex-F-Interruptions-v1.1.pdf. **verified**. Supports:
  - §2.1: the list of measures (CI, CML, SI, RI, ONI).
  - §2.2: NaFIRS (run by ENA).
  - §2.7: an incident is ≥ 3 minutes.
  - §2.26–2.27: interruption sequences, with the worked example 2 + 2 + 30 → 34 minutes.
  - §2.28: multi-phase customers.
  - §2.30–2.33: short interruptions, multi-shot reclosing, restoration stages.
- [OP-03] *Information and Incentives Project – Draft Regulatory Instructions and Guidance version 5* (Ofgem, March 2004). https://www.ofgem.gov.uk/sites/default/files/docs/2004/03/6621-67b04.pdf. **verified**. Supports §2.3, the formulas:
  - CI per 100 customers.
  - CML = Σ customer minutes lost over all restoration stages / total customers.
  - SI per 100 customers.
  - RI per 100 customers.
  - The 3-minute threshold.
  - Note: this is the historical version (in effect from 1 Apr 2005).
- [OP-20] IEEE 1366-2022, retailer listing (Nimonik). https://www.standards.nimonik.com/products/ieee/ieee-1366/. **snippet-only**. Supports: 1366-2022 is the current edition (published 22 Nov 2022). I did not obtain the standard's text.
- [OP-21] AEMC EPR0041 *Distribution Reliability Measures* final report, and the AER *Distribution Reliability Measures Guideline*. https://www.aemc.gov.au/sites/default/files/content/792bdac4-bfec-45a4-9a95-d4cc8f710db7/EPR0041-Distribution-Reliability-Measures-Final-Report-sent-to-COAG-Energy-Council.PDF and https://www.aer.gov.au/system/files/AER%20-%20Distribution%20Reliability%20Measures%20Guideline%20-%20Version%201%20-%2014%20November%202018%20%28updated%2020%20November%202018%29.pdf. **snippet-only**. Supports: Australia moved the momentary threshold from < 1 min to < 3 min, and the AER uses the IEEE MED method with 2.5 log standard deviations.
- [OP-40] Gamma Electronics mirror of a power-systems course page, "Distribution Reliability" (2012). https://gammaelectronics.xyz/industrial-electricity-com/pwr_syst_analysis_dsgn_15g.html. **verified but uncertain** (anonymous mirror with garbled formulas). Supports:

## Restoration / FLISR / fault management

- [OP-04] *Fault Location, Isolation, and Service Restoration Technologies Reduce Outage Impact and Duration* (U.S. DOE, SGIG program, Dec 2014). https://www.energy.gov/sites/prod/files/2016/10/f33/Fault_Location_Impact_Duration_Dec_2014.pdf. **verified**. Supports:
  - 266 FLISR operations (Apr 2013 – Mar 2014).
  - About 270,000 fewer customers interrupted and about 38 million fewer customer-minutes.
  - CI reduced by up to 45 % and CMI by up to 51 %.
  - Automated actions take less than 1 min; manually validated actions take 5 min or more.
  - Tables 4–7: full and partial feeder outages; operator-initiated vs fully automated (CMI −47 % / −53 %, CI −35 % / −55 %).
  - Note: Table 5's column header says "CI" but the values are CMI. This is a typo in the source.
- [OP-05] *PR-NET-OSM-064 Management of Faults on High Voltage Underground Cables – Operational Safety Manual Section 8.3*, Rev 1.00 (SSEN Distribution, March 2023). https://ssen.co.uk/globalassets/safety/s8/pr-net-osm-064-management-of-faults-on-high-voltage-underground-cables---operational-safety-manual---section-8.3-rev1.00.pdf. **verified**. Supports:
  - §1.2: purpose (minimise CML safely).
  - §8: hazards.
  - §9.2: safety over pressure to restore.
  - §11: FPIs, including positive vs negative indication.
  - §12: auto-reclose disabled on all-cable circuits.
  - §13: sectionalising rules (re-energising delays, mid-point split by customer numbers, quarter points, operation counts, exclusion of staff from substations, 15 m distance at grid/primary).
  - §14: locate the fault, then isolate and earth it.
  - §17: third-party damage.
- [OP-06] *PR-NET-OSM-014 Response to System Faults – OSM Section 2.4*, Rev 1.00 (SSEN, March 2023). https://ssen.co.uk/globalassets/safety/s2/pr-net-osm-014-response-to-system-faults--operational-safety-manual---section-2.4-rev1.00.pdf. **verified**. Supports:
  - §9: fault-level reduction (150 MVA at 11 kV, 100 MVA at 6.6 kV).
  - §10, Table 10.1: re-energising delays (5 min at night, 20 min by day, at 6.6–33 kV).
  - §11: manual reclosing risk balance (ENA SHE Standard 06).
  - §12: Fault Action Plan, halving by customer numbers, restoring from an alternative source, proving the last section by re-energisation or a pressure test within 12 h, remaining fault-breaking capacity, injury reports taking priority.
  - §13: FPI-driven automation logic.
- [OP-31] GE P4A technical manual, introduction to autoreclose (ManualsLib). https://www.manualslib.com/manual/1331120/Ge-P4a.html?page=152. **snippet-only**. Supports: reclosing onto a faulty cable is likely to make the damage worse.
- [OP-22] IPART (NSW) Safety Alert, 9 Dec 2019. https://www.ipart.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/documents/ipart-safety-alert-20191209-1b_0.pdf. **snippet-only** (fetch returned 503). Supports: an incident where a switch was energised with upstream auto-reclose still active; the recommendation to disable auto-reclose for switching; preference for remote switching.
- [OP-33] arXiv 2501.06030, author preprint for IEEE PES GM 2025 (ComEd-related work on crew dispatch). https://arxiv.org/html/2501.06030v2. **snippet-only**. Supports: the restoration-phase sequence (patrol, then tree crew, then construction crew) and travel time in dispatch.
- [OP-34] *Outages of electric power supply resulting from cable failures, Boston Edison Company system* (OSTI 5083196). https://www.osti.gov/biblio/5083196. **snippet-only**. Supports: a historical incident report whose recommendations include a network outage restoration plan.

## Commissioning tests

- [OP-16] A. Schröder, M. Neuser, M. Nagelschmidt (Westnetz), H. Putter, R. Probst (Megger), *Widescale VLF testing statistics over the last decade at Westnetz GmbH*, Jicable'23, paper C2-4 (2023). https://jicable.org/TOUT_JICABLE_FIRST_PAGE/2023/2023-C2-4_page1.pdf. **verified** (page 1). Supports:
  - IEC 60502-2 (2014) recommends 3·U0, 15 min, 0.1 Hz.
  - DIN VDE 0276-620 recommends 3·U0, 60 min.
  - Westnetz uses 30 min (since 2014).
  - DC testing was banned internally at Westnetz for polymer cables from 1997 because of space-charge problems.
  - Outer-sheath test at 5 kV for 5–10 min.
- [OP-17] *On Site Testing Guidelines for MV Cables* (Prysmian Group UK, 2019). https://uk.prysmian.com/sites/uk.prysmian.com/files/media/documents/On%20Site%20Testing%20Guidelines%20%282019%29%20%283%29.pdf. **verified**. Supports:
  - IEC 60502-2 §20.3.1 options: U (phase-to-phase) for 15 min at 20–300 Hz; U0 for 24 h; 3U0 at 0.1 Hz for 15 min.
  - §20.3.2: DC 4U0 for 15 min, with a caution about DC.
  - Expected IR above 10,000 MΩ·km at 20 °C.
- [OP-18] IEC 60502-2 Ed. 3.0 (2014-02) preview sample (iTeh). https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/19152/259c88bf5129473d934021d3d3d15e0c/IEC-60502-2-2014.pdf. **verified** (foreword and contents only). Supports: clause 20 is "Electrical tests after installation", and the 2014 edition change "g) the inclusion of a 0,1Hz test after installation".

## Planning / economics

- [OP-19] Ofgem decision DCRP/22/03, *EREC P2 Issue 8 – Security of Supply* (31 Jan 2023; implemented 1 Jul 2023). https://www.ofgem.gov.uk/sites/default/files/2023-02/FINAL_DCRP2203%20Engineering%20Recommendation%20P2.pdf. **verified**. Supports:
  - P2 sets the minimum demand to be restored within set times.
  - An HV feeder is generally Class B (1–12 MW).
  - Class B changed from "within 3 hours group demand minus 1 MW" to "group demand minus 1.2 MW" for circuits of 1 km or less meeting EREP 130 criteria.
  - The criteria cap added time off supply at 10 min per customer per year.
  - VoLL-based cost-benefit analysis (Imperial College, 2016).
- [OP-23] Transformer TOC: *Impact of transformer capitalization factors on the carbon footprint* (Transformers Magazine, https://transformers-magazine.com/files/Impact-of-transformer-capitalization-factors-on-the-carbon-footprint.pdf) and Copper Development Association, *Introduction to Transformer Losses* (https://archive.copper.org/environment/sustainable-energy/transformers/education/trans_losses.php). **snippet-only**. Supports:
  - TOC = Cpp + A·P0 + B·Pk, where A and B are the present value per watt of no-load and load loss.
  - The example A = $7,500/kW and B = $2,500/kW is illustrative only.
- [OP-24] VoLL: London Economics for Ofgem/DECC (2013), via the ENWL VoLL literature review, and the ENWL VoLL2 summary. https://www.enwl.co.uk/globalassets/innovation/enwl010-voll/voll-methodology/voll-methodology-statement-addendum-a-literature-review.pdf. **snippet-only**. Supports:
  - £16,940/MWh domestic + SME average (London Economics).
  - RIIO-ED1 used £16,000/MWh.
  - Literature range: a few €/kWh up to €45/kWh for households.
- [OP-25] *Electricity Safety, Quality and Continuity Regulations 2002*, reg. 27 (legislation.gov.uk). https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2002/2665/regulation/27/made. **snippet-only**. Supports: LV is 230 V +10 % / −6 %, and HV below 132 kV is ±6 % (UK).
- [OP-29] World Bank indicator EG.ELC.LOSS.ZS (T&D losses, % of output), via the IndexMundi mirror. https://www.indexmundi.com/facts/saudi-arabia/electric-power-transmission-and-distribution-losses. **snippet-only**. Supports: Saudi Arabia 6.78 % (2014), which is old.
- [OP-30] *Electricity (Standards of Performance) Regulations 2015*, reg. 5 (UK), as described in the ScottishPower and National Grid customer notices. https://www.scottishpower.com/userfiles/document_library/GuaranteedStandardsNoticeofRights2015.pdf. **snippet-only**. Supports: restore within 12 h in normal weather, or a payment is due.
- [OP-32] UniSA *RP3017 After Diversity Demand report* (Lochiel Park). https://unisa.edu.au/siteassets/research/node-lcl/research-projects/published---rp3017-after-diversity-demand-report---final.pdf. **snippet-only**. Supports: ADMD per household = precinct maximum demand / number of households (Australian, not Saudi).
